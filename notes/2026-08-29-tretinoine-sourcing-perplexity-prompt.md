# Sourcing tretinoin (topical retinoic acid) for delivery to Quebec, Canada — best price with reliable customs clearance

## Objective

I need to identify the most cost-effective and reliable sources for **topical tretinoin cream or gel (all-trans retinoic acid)** shipped to **Quebec, Canada (Montréal)**, for personal long-term use on a large body surface (abdomen, décolleté, breasts). Annual consumption estimated at **8–12 tubes of 30 g** at 0.025% and 0.05% concentrations. I am not looking for retinol OTC or adapalene; specifically tretinoin.

## Hard constraints

1. **Delivery to Canada (Quebec)** with a documented track record of successful delivery to Canadian addresses within the past 12 months.
2. **CBSA (Canada Border Services Agency) customs behavior**: sources whose shipments clear customs predictably. Personal importation of prescription drugs into Canada exists in a grey zone (Health Canada's Personal Importation Policy allows up to 3-month supply for personal use, but enforcement varies). I want sources known to declare or package in ways that minimize seizure risk, and I want to know **which sources have documented seizure incidents in recent months**.
3. **Preferred: no Canadian prescription required** at the source (many overseas pharmacies ship without one). If a source requires prescription, note it clearly.
4. **Total cost per tube delivered** (including shipping, any reshipment guarantee, currency conversion) is the primary metric.
5. **Product authenticity**: source must sell genuine branded generics (Retino-A by Janssen-Cilag India / Menarini, A-Ret by Menarini, Tretin by Cipla, Retacnyl by Galderma, Ketrel, Locacid, etc.), not unbranded compounded products of unknown provenance.

## Deliverables

### 1. Comparative table (top 6–10 sources)
For each source provide:
- Site name and URL
- Country of dispatch
- Available brands, concentrations (0.025%, 0.05%, 0.1%), tube sizes
- Unit price per 30 g tube
- Shipping cost (standard and express) to Canada
- Minimum order value
- Reshipment/refund policy if customs seizes
- Estimated delivery time to Quebec
- Payment methods accepted
- Prescription required? (yes/no/optional)
- Years in operation and general reputation
- **Recent Canadian customer feedback** (Reddit r/tretinoin, r/CanadianPharmacy, r/canadaskincareaddicts, Trustpilot, ResellerRatings within past 12 months)
- **Recent CBSA seizure reports** if any (Reddit, forums, complaint sites)

- **Compounding pharmacies in Quebec** (préparations magistrales): typical cost of custom-compounded tretinoin cream, quality considerations vs commercial generics.
- **Mexican pharmacies with international shipping**: is Retacnyl or Neotretin from Mexican sources a viable channel?

### 4. Legal and practical notes
- Current status of Health Canada Personal Importation Policy for prescription drugs in 2026
- Documented experiences of Canadian recipients: what happens when a shipment is intercepted (letter, return, seizure, fine, referral)
- Best practices for packaging, declaration, quantity per shipment to reduce customs friction
- Any regulatory shifts in 2025-2026 affecting personal importation of dermatological prescriptions

### 5. Final recommendation
Rank the top 2–3 sources by **total landed cost per tube + reliability + reshipment guarantee**, and give a suggested order pattern (single large order vs staggered smaller shipments) to optimize cost while minimizing customs exposure.

## Search focus
Prioritize sources dated **2025–2026** (Reddit threads, Trustpilot reviews, blog posts). Older references are useful for context but not for pricing or reliability decisions. Cross-check price claims against the source's current live website. Flag any information older than 18 months explicitly.
