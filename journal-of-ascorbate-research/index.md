# The Journal of Ascorbate Research

<p class="chapo">A documentary and critical review of research into ascorbate, in its biochemical, clinical, historical, and archival dimensions. Companion publication to the <a href="{{ '/rights/vitamin-c/' | relative_url }}">Right to Adequate Vitamin C Status</a>.</p>

---

## Scope

The *Journal* collects what the fiche on vitamin C cannot absorb without losing its line: archaeological recoveries of neglected literature, biographical reconstructions of clinicians and researchers who carried the dossier before its institutional abandonment, examinations of controversies that were closed by social means rather than scientific ones, and documentary assembly of the ascorbate corpus as it continues to be dispersed across journals, archives, and expiring websites.

It is not a vehicle for primary research. It is a vehicle for the *rehabilitation of primary research already conducted*, much of which has been stranded by the collapse of institutional interest between the 1970s and today. The editorial wager is that a rigorous reading of what already exists yields more progress than a thousand new underpowered trials.
