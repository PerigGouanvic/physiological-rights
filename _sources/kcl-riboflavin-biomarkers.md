      - "Erythrocyte glutathione reductase activity coefficient (EGRAC) This assay has been commonly used to determine riboflavin adequacy. The enzyme activity of the erythrocyte glutathione reductase is measured before and after exposure to FAD. The results are expressed as EGRAC; an EGRAC of 1.0 indicates no stimulation by FAD due to more than adequate riboflavin status (4, 7). The IOM suggest the following interpretation of EGRAC: <1.2 is acceptable, 1.2-1.4 is low, >1.4 is deficient. This assay cannot be used for individuals with glucose 6-phosphate dehydrogenase  (G6PD) deficiency due to the resulting increased avidity of EGR towards FAD (4)."
