      - "The good news is that this extra burden of disease can be avoided: by adding magnesium back into desalinated water, it is possible to reduce illness rates and save the healthcare system hundreds of millions of shekels. The expected annual savings in direct healthcare expenses from preventing type 2 diabetes and ischemic stroke, resulting from increasing magnesium intake by 50 mg per day, range from NIS 83 to 188 million in 2025 and between NIS 110 to 253 million in 2040."
