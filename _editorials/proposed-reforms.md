# Proposed Reforms

On the basis of documented cases and analysis of physiological rights, the following systemic reforms are proposed:

### 1. Legal Recognition of Physiological Rights
Integrate physiological rights into national and international legal frameworks, as a complement to existing rights.

### 2. Universal Access to Physiological Testing
Guarantee free and systematic access to tests measuring essential physiological parameters (levels of vitamins, minerals, hormones).

### 3. Reimbursement of Supplements and Corrective Treatments
Ensure public reimbursement of nutritional supplements and hormonal treatments necessary to correct measured physiological imbalances.

### 4. Training of Health Professionals
Train physicians and other health professionals to recognise the importance of optimal physiological parameters and to adopt a proactive approach to prevention and correction of imbalances.

### 5. Research on Individualised Norms
Fund research on individualised physiological norms, rather than simple population averages.
