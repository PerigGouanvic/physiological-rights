# Physiological Rights: The Full Set of Essential Needs

**Physiological rights** represent the full set of physiological needs that must be actively met to enable a person to live fully. These rights focus specifically on the right to the maintenance and restoration of physiological integrity when it has been compromised — whether through neglect, illness, or environmental factors.

They occupy a place within the interdependent network of human rights, complementing without replacing the right to health, the right to food, and other related rights.

Unlike broader concepts, physiological rights focus on concrete and measurable parameters of the organism: essential nutrient levels, hormonal balance, and the proper functioning of vital systems. Their recognition is crucial for a holistic and effective approach to health and well-being.
