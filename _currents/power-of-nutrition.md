# The Power of Nutrition

Independent charitable foundation launched in 2015 by a consortium including the U.K. Department for International Development, the Children's Investment Fund Foundation and UBS Optimus Foundation. Pools private and public capital to scale evidence-based nutrition interventions in low- and middle-income countries, with an operational focus on the first 1,000 days.

## Why it matters here

The Power of Nutrition is one of the models the *Dispensary* piece in this resource is written against and, in part, in dialogue with: an institution that has managed to attract serious philanthropic capital to a nutritional object, and that has organized itself around the international-development definition of that object. The question for the resource is whether the same institutional form is usable for a physiological object defined at higher physiological standards and in richer countries.
