# Alexandra Finch

Associate at the O'Neill Institute for National and Global Health Law at Georgetown. Works on global health law, the international regulation of health emergencies, and the human rights dimensions of pandemic preparedness and equitable access to medical countermeasures.

## Why she matters here

Finch belongs to the next generation of scholars at the O'Neill Institute, the institution that most directly shapes the North American vocabulary of the right to health. The younger cohort of scholars is often, in practice, where new arguments first receive a receptive hearing.
