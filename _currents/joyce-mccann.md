# Joyce C. McCann

Research scientist and long-time collaborator of Bruce Ames. Co-authored the series of systematic reviews that translated the Triage Theory from a hypothesis into a body of evidence, testing the theory nutrient by nutrient: vitamin K, vitamin D, selenium and others, each time showing that the functions preserved under marginal intake are the short-term functions, and that the long-term functions (chromosomal integrity, mitochondrial maintenance, immune surveillance) are the ones that quietly give way.

## Why she matters here

McCann is the methodological link between the Ames laboratory and the operational program this resource proposes. Her reviews establish that the Triage Theory is not a metaphor but a discipline, one that can be applied to any nutrient for which a hierarchy of functions is known.
