# Lancet Commission on Investing in Health

Commission first reporting in 2013 (*Global Health 2035*) and again in 2024 (*Global Health 2050*), successor to the 1993 World Bank report *Investing in Health* that established the disability-adjusted life year (DALY) as the international currency of health-policy analysis.

## Why it matters here

This Commission is the institutional custodian of the DALY framework, the framework in which international health decisions are argued and defended. A physiological rights argument that names, as its object, biological parameters not currently costed in DALY analyses is asking, at bottom, for a revision of the accounting scheme the Commission maintains. That is the structural adjacency to name.
