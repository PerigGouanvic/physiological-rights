# Frank B. Hu

Chair of the Department of Nutrition at Harvard's T.H. Chan School of Public Health. Principal investigator on the Nurses' Health Study and the Health Professionals Follow-up Study, from which much of the current cardiovascular and diabetic epidemiology of diet is drawn.

## Why he matters here

Hu represents the center of gravity of the mainstream nutritional epidemiology this resource does not oppose but does situate: population-level dietary patterns, established in large cohorts, do give a first-order account of chronic disease burden, and yet they do not, and cannot, tell an individual patient whether her own tissue magnesium or her own 25(OH)D concentration lies in a range compatible with mitochondrial or immune health. The two levels of description are complementary, and it is the second that this resource insists is missing.
