# Aeyal Gross

Professor of Law at Tel Aviv University and, at various points, visiting professor at SOAS, University of London. Author of the monograph *The Right to Health at the Public/Private Divide: A Global Comparative Study*, which examines the way in which the right to health, formally recognized in the ICESCR and in domestic constitutions, is in practice repurposed by health-system marketization.

## Why he matters here

Gross's argument is precisely the one this resource echoes on the biological side: that a right that is formally recognized but not operationalized, or that is operationalized in a way that inverts its purpose, is not a right in the sense that the covenants meant. His comparative account is a natural interlocutor for the definitions collection of this resource.
