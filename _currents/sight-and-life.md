# Sight and Life Foundation

Nutrition-focused foundation historically funded by DSM Nutritional Products (now dsm-firmenich) and organized around the elimination of vitamin A deficiency, iron-deficiency anemia and the wider problem of hidden hunger in low- and middle-income countries. Publishes a house magazine and a series of technical reports that circulate in the WHO, UNICEF and World Food Programme networks.

## Why it matters here

Sight and Life produces some of the most detailed English-language material on the specific micronutrients this resource is concerned with, and it does so with a commercial interest in the vitamin premix market. That combination, technical seriousness paired with an unavoidable commercial position, is the paradigmatic form of the structural adjacency this axis of the collection is trying to name.
